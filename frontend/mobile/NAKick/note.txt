User Flow Design
    Flow 1: Browse Products → Add to Cart
1. User browses products
   ↓
2. Clicks "Add to Cart"
   ↓
3. Show loading spinner
   ↓
4. Update cart icon badge
   ↓
5. Show success toast/notification
   ↓
6. Optionally open cart drawer
    Flow 2: View Cart
1. Click cart icon
   ↓
2. Open cart drawer/navigate to cart page
   ↓
3. Load cart data
   ↓
4. Display items with quantities
   ↓
5. Show summary with subtotal/tax/total
    Flow 3: Modify Cart
1. Change quantity (+/- buttons)
   ↓
2. Real-time price update
   ↓
3. Save to backend
   ↓
4. Update cart summary
   ↓
5. Show loading state during save
